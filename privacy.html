02

Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Niz processes personal data when you use the Niz application, keyboard extension, website and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Niz is intended only for individuals aged 18 or older.

03

How Niz works

Niz helps you compose replies to conversations.

When you request a suggestion, you may choose to provide one or more screenshots containing a conversation. Niz processes the content of those screenshots in order to understand the context of the conversation and generate suggested replies.

Niz does not continuously read or monitor the content displayed on your screen.

Nothing generated by Niz is automatically sent to another person. You remain responsible for choosing whether to use, modify or send a suggested reply.

04

Personal data we process

Depending on what appears in a screenshot you choose to provide, Niz may temporarily process:

  • written messages and conversation content;
  • names, usernames or profile names;
  • profile pictures or other images;
  • dates, times and other information visible in the screenshot;
  • any other information that you or another person has included in the captured conversation.

Because you determine what appears in the screenshots you provide, they may contain personal data relating both to you and to other individuals.

We ask you not to provide screenshots containing information that is unnecessary for the generation of a reply, particularly highly sensitive information.

At present, Niz does not require you to create a user account.

Niz does not currently use advertising trackers or third-party analytics tools.

05

Why we process this data

Conversation screenshots and their contents are processed for the purpose of providing the functionality you request: understanding the relevant conversation context and generating suggested replies.

Where required under applicable law, processing is based on your consent and/or is necessary to provide the service that you have explicitly requested.

You decide whether and when to submit a screenshot for processing.

06

Use of artificial intelligence and OpenAI

To generate suggested replies, Niz uses artificial intelligence services provided by OpenAI.

Important information about AI processing

When you request an AI-generated reply, the screenshot(s) and/or information extracted from them may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing. This can include personal data relating to you or other individuals appearing in the conversation.

This means that conversation content, including personal data relating to you or other individuals appearing in a screenshot, may be processed by OpenAI for the purpose of generating the requested response.

Niz does not authorize OpenAI to use API inputs and outputs for training its models. According to OpenAI's current API data policies, API inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI models by default unless the API customer explicitly opts in.

OpenAI may nevertheless retain certain API inputs and outputs for a limited period for purposes including service provision, security and abuse prevention, subject to OpenAI's applicable data retention policies and the particular API services used.

You will be informed that content is transmitted to OpenAI and, where required, asked for permission before such transmission occurs.

07

Screenshot storage and retention

Niz does not maintain a stored archive of the screenshots you submit for AI generation.

Screenshots are processed for the purpose of fulfilling your generation request and are not intentionally persisted by Niz as part of a conversation history.

However, information transmitted to OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's own processing and retention practices. OpenAI currently states that, for most API services, API inputs and outputs may be retained for up to 30 days for service operation and abuse monitoring, unless a different retention arrangement applies.

We may also retain information where required to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute or protect our legal rights.

08

Data relating to other people

Screenshots you provide may contain information about another person, such as a conversation partner.

You should only provide content that you are lawfully entitled to use and should avoid submitting unnecessary, highly sensitive, confidential or private information.

You are responsible for the content you choose to submit through Niz.

Niz is designed to minimize processing by allowing you to choose when and what content is submitted for AI processing.
